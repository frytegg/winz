# Findings log

Kept at every rubric weighting, because it is where the idea comes from before it is ever a
deliverable. See `references/01-find-the-gap.md`.

A finding is a **disagreement between two layers** — spec, implementation, SDK, docs, or a
reasonable developer assumption. It is not a finding until all four layers have been
checked.

## Translating a finding into a product

For each one, in writing:

- **Who loses?** A named role, not "users".
- **How much, and how would they find out?** Put a number on it. If they would never find
  out, that is a bigger finding.
- **Who benefits from it staying invisible?** A party with discretion means an asymmetry,
  and asymmetries make the best products.

The biggest translated finding is your product.
