# Known behaviour

One line per verified fact, with its evidence. The antidote to folklore: every project
accumulates confident beliefs within two days, and this is what stops the team — and the
assistant — re-asserting something nobody executed.

A fact does not go in here from a document. It goes in from the implementation source plus
an execution. See `references/02-verification.md` for the evidence ladder.
