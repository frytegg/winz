## Summary

- **Corpus.** 8 projects, scanned statically as 10 sub-corpora (about 46,100 authored lines in the 9 shipped sub-corpora; 46,507 with the raw SUI export). Authorship varies: 2 were generated by Figma Make (SUI, BaseBatches), 2 were mostly built by a teammate, Andrea (XRPL, ETHDenver), 1 grew from the sponsor's template (Hedera), and 3 are mostly the owner's own work (BNB, inflexion, Monad `web/`). The files are the evidence; nothing was run.
- **Where the hits are.** Across the 20 tells I count 30 full hits (corrected at verification from 33). 19 of them (63%) come from the two Figma Make exports: SUI 9, BaseBatches 10. ETHDenver, built on React Bits drop-ins, has 5. The other five projects have 0 to 2 each.
  - "Every frontend screamed vibecoding" holds strongly for 3 projects out of 8. For the other 5, the static evidence is weak.
- **The most widespread tell is copy, not visuals.** Tell 16 (em dashes) exceeds /prose PR-01's 2.0 per 1,000 words in 5 of 8 projects on raw counts (inflexion 17.72, BNB 12.30, Monad 5.03, XRPL 4.64, ETHDenver 2.93), and in 4 of 8 once the title-separator and placeholder exclusions proposed in F06 are applied (inflexion 17.48, BNB 10.89, XRPL 3.11, ETHDenver 2.93; Monad web falls to 1.89).
  - It is near zero in the Figma exports (0 and 0.70) and low in Hedera (1.00).
  - Next most frequent: glassmorphism and coloured-border cards (3 of 8 each).
- **Five tells never fully fire here:** emoji in headings (T3), Inter everywhere (T4), serif italic accents (T18), the Space Grotesk + Instrument Serif pair (T19) and grain over a *chromatic* gradient (T20). T3 and T4 each have one partial: BaseBatches renders data-driven game emoji beside a page `<h1>`, and SUI requests Inter but most likely never applies it (F26). Space Grotesk alone appears in Monad and in inflexion's first scaffold. The checker cannot be tuned to "fire here" on these five; it needs synthetic positives.
- **Count what renders, not what is defined.** 5 colour-gradient classes and 1 theme block are defined but never rendered, 8 of ETHDenver's 16 vendored effect components never render, and 1 class used on an `<h1>` is never defined.
  - Rules must count rendered usages and ignore comments. A stale comment produced a false Space Grotesk hit.
- **Sponsor-brand false positive.** Hedera's purple-to-blue gradient (`#8259ef` to `#0031ff`) is the sponsor's own brand, shipped in `hedera-dev/scaffold-hbar`. T1 needs a sponsor-brand allowlist.
- **Spacing metrics are inverted by stack.** The four Tailwind corpora show 13 to 16 spacing steps with 77% to 85% of uses in their top 5 values. Hand-written CSS shows 23 to 34 distinct px values, the winner included. Static spacing diversity measures the styling approach, not consistency, so T15 must be checked on the rendered page.
- **Low contrast (T7), computed from tokens:**
  - ETHDenver's muted text is 2.19:1 (40 text-colour uses).
  - BaseBatches' `muted-foreground/30` is 1.62:1.
  - inflexion's tertiary text is 4.14 to 4.56:1 (103 uses).
- **Inter (T4) is requested in 1 project only, SUI, and there it most likely never renders:** Figma Make wraps the `body` Inter rule in a guard selector that fails on any page using spaced `text-` utilities (F26). Two strong human-designed controls do use Inter (tailwindcss.com `'Inter var'`, Uniswap `@fontsource/inter`), so T4 cannot be a tier-1 rule.
- **XRPL (first place) did several things right:**
  - a frontend spec that names a visual reference and bans effects;
  - 12 named tokens and a motion budget;
  - shadcn rethemed, with its `.dark` block and Geist font removed;
  - a vocabulary audit, and a runtime `ui-audit.js` (DESIGN.md:194: "Environ **70 vérifications**", about 70 checks) that is direct prior art for /frontend.
- **But the XRPL win is weak evidence for its frontend.** Presentation was 10% of the score (owner's memory note), and 45 of its 63 frontend commits are by the teammate who also built the effect-heavy ETHDenver frontend.
- **Control corpus (part d).** 11 public repositories are pinned at pre-2023 commits and attributed to designers or design teams; 2 of them match the negative corpus's stack (briOS, tailwindcss.com). Companion scanner: `_scripts/fd14/internal-negative-corpus.scan.py`; its full final output is in `evidence/internal-negative-corpus/scan-final-2026-09-30.txt`.

## Weak spots of this dossier

- **Static only.** Nothing was rendered. Layout rhythm, hierarchy, density, image choice and motion *feel* are invisible here, and they may carry much of the owner's "vibecoding" impression. T15 and the truth of T7 are explicitly out of reach.
- **Heuristic copy extraction.** Word counts include some code text (BNB's inline client script); JSX text split around expressions was only partly recovered. One fix moved XRPL's em-dash rate from 5.51 to 4.64. Rates are approximate to about ±20%.
- **The ●/◐/○ symbols are my reading of each hit in context.** The counts are reproducible; the judgements are not mechanical.
- **Small, lopsided corpus.** n = 8, with two generator exports supplying 63% of the full hits (19 of 30 after verification). Per-project numbers are not statistically meaningful, and 5 tells have no full positive at all (T3 and T4 have one partial each).
- **Mixed authorship.** XRPL and ETHDenver frontends are mostly a teammate's work; Monad's mockups are another teammate's; Hedera's visuals are the sponsor's. "The owner's frontends" should be read as "frontends of the owner's teams".
- **Final states, not event states.** Git history shows tells being removed (XRPL) and fonts being swapped (inflexion). What judges saw may differ from what was scanned.
- **Working trees.** SUI numbers come from a working tree with 3 uncommitted files, all three inside the scanned path (one UI file, two non-UI modules).
- **Declarations, not computed styles.** Verification found two declared-but-inert tells that the original static pass scored as full hits (SUI's guarded Inter rule, ETHDenver's blur under an opaque fill). Other ● judgements rest on the same kind of reading and may hide similar cases until the Playwright pass exists.
- **Arbitrary constants.** Hue bands, saturation floors, the 10-line badge window and the phrase list are my choices, and they must be calibrated on the control corpus, which has not been run yet.
- **Monad's mockups are French.** They were measured with rules calibrated on English prose.
