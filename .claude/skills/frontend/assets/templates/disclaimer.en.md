# Concept mockup disclaimer (English)

## Where it goes

On the mockup frame itself, as a visible label marked `data-concept-label` (the ip.concept-label gate looks for it),
not only in a footer. It also appears as on-screen text in every video shot where the mockup is visible, and in a
"Third-party names" section of the README. A notice that stays behind when the image travels does not protect
(Arsenal, FD09-F14).

## What it does not do

It does not license a copy of anyone's design, text or images (FD09-F01, F07). It does not cure a presentation that
already suggests a commercial link (FD09-F13, F14). It works only on top of policy rows 7 and 9: the company named in
plain text only, an original generic frame in the project's own design, fictional data, no "powered by" claim, and a
fictional stand-in whenever the name adds nothing.
